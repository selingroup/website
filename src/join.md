We welcome students and postdocs from a range of backgrounds (atmospheric science, engineering, data science, economics, and policy) who are interested in pollution, modeling, and sustainability science.

## For prospective students

Students who work in our group are in two different departments at MIT:

- [Institute for Data, Systems and Society](https://idss.mit.edu) (including the PhD program in Social and Engineering Systems and the Master of Science in Technology and Policy)
- [Department of Earth, Atmospheric and Planetary Sciences](https://eapsweb.mit.edu)

Application deadlines are in December for admission the following September.

## Other collaborators
We do not host unpaid interns or volunteer researchers. Prospective UROPs who are current MIT undergraduates are encouraged to reach out to us directly. 

## Finding us

Our offices are in several locations on campus, including Buildings E17-E18, E19, and 54. The MIT campus is easily accessible from the Red Line Kendall Square subway stop. For directions on campus, see [whereis.mit.edu](https://whereis.mit.edu).
