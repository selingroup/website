The winners of the first-ever Climate Grand Challenges (CGC) will become multiyear flagship research projects, helping define a new research agenda focused on addressing complex unsolved climate problems and bringing high-impact solutions to the world on an accelerated basis.

Prof. Selin co-leads the project Bringing Computation to the Climate Challenge with Prof. Raffaele Ferrari (EAPS).

Read more at [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2022/flagship-projects-climate-grand-challenges-0411), and more about the [Bringing Computation to the Climate Challenge project](https://climategrandchallenges.mit.edu/flagship-projects/bringing-computation-to-the-climate-challenge/).
