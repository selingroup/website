Jamie graduated from the University of Edinburgh (U.K.) with a PhD in Atmospheric and Environmental Sciences. During this time, Jamie developed the chemistry scheme within a global atmospheric model. Using this new chemistry scheme, Jamie examined how future changes in climate and emissions will alter the distributions of organic aerosols in the atmosphere. Now at MIT, Jamie's role in the [Superfund Research Program](https://superfund.mit.edu) (SRP) is to model Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs), with particular emphasis on their sources, transport, transformations, and health impacts.
