I am a third year doctoral student at MIT’s Institute for Data, Systems, and Society, working in the Center for Sustainability Science and Strategy with Dr. Noelle Selin.

My research interests are in humanist & holistic approaches to data science for climate and energy science, particularly as it relates to environmental justice and urban sustainability. I currently work in socio-ecological network theory, developing case studies for energy transition in Alberta, Canada, and data center expansion in Texas.

I graduated from Smith College magna cum laude with a Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics and a minor in Environmental Science & Policy. My undergraduate thesis advised by Dr. Luca Capogna and Dr. Elisa Negrini, System Identification with Neural Networks in the Smith Geothermal Project, received highest honours.

I spend my free time hiking, camping, and playing music.
