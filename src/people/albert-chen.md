Albert (Yuang) Chen studies how climate and energy policy decisions shape air quality and public health. His research focuses on organizational climate actions and growing electricity demand from data centers, examining how they shift pollution across space, time, and sectors and who experiences the resulting health impacts. He combines energy systems modeling, atmospheric chemistry and transport modeling, health impact assessment, and statistical methods to connect changes in energy use to emissions, population exposure, and health. Before joining MIT, Albert earned his bachelor’s degree in environmental science at Peking University. In his free time, he enjoys playing ultimate frisbee.
