Andrew's research interests lie at the intersection of transportation, energy systems, and atmospheric science. Andrew is also enthusiastic about pedagogy, working as an instructor at Wentworth Institute of Technology and assisting in the delivery of MIT courses sponsored by NASA Goddard, Blue Origin, and others. In his doctoral work, he is assessing the climate and air quality impacts of replacing flights with passenger rail travel in the United States. Andrew completed his master's degree with the MIT Gas Turbine Laboratory, exploring the design space of hydrogen-fueled propulsion systems for electrified civil aircraft. Prior to attending MIT, he received a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from Penn State University. In his free time, Andrew enjoys cycling, local music, watching football (Go Birds!), and playing video games.
