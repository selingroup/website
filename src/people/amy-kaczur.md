Amy Kaczur is the Selin Group Administrator at the Institute for Data, Systems, and Society (IDSS) at MIT, a role she has held since joining the Institute in January 2017. Her administrative career spans higher education, the arts, and the culinary industry, bringing a diverse background in organizational operations, program support, communications, and community engagement.

Outside of her professional work at MIT, Amy is a video artist whose projects explore environmental issues, especially vulnerable salt marshes along the U.S. East Coast. She holds degrees from Tufts University and the University of California, Irvine, and has lived in Ohio, California, and Massachusetts. In her free time, she enjoys gardening, cooking, hiking, and documenting the outdoors on video. Her email is akaczur at mit.
